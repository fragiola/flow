---
title: Controls
description: Flow.Controls groups the viewport's buttons (zoom in, zoom out, fit view, lock) or your own, placed and named by you.
---

`Flow.Controls` renders a `div` inside `Flow.Root`, outside `Flow.Viewport`, for the viewport's
buttons: `Flow.ZoomIn`, `Flow.ZoomOut`, `Flow.FitView`, `Flow.ToggleInteractive`, or your own
calling the engine. Presses and the wheel over it are left to it. It has no role and no name:
give it a group role and a label, and place it with your style.

```tsx
import { MaximizeIcon, MinusIcon, PlusIcon } from "lucide-react";

<Flow.Controls role="group" aria-label="Zoom" className="absolute bottom-3 left-3 flex flex-col">
    <Flow.ZoomIn aria-label="Zoom in" className="p-1 data-disabled:opacity-40">
        <PlusIcon />
    </Flow.ZoomIn>
    <Flow.ZoomOut aria-label="Zoom out" className="p-1 data-disabled:opacity-40">
        <MinusIcon />
    </Flow.ZoomOut>
    <Flow.FitView aria-label="Fit view" className="p-1">
        <MaximizeIcon />
    </Flow.FitView>
</Flow.Controls>;
```

## Props

`ControlsProps` extends the [common primitive props](/docs/api/primitives/root#common-props) and
every `div` attribute.

| prop | type | default | description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `children` | `ReactNode` | none | the buttons |

## State

`ControlsState` is empty: its buttons have their own state.

The buttons `Flow.ZoomIn`, `Flow.ZoomOut` and `Flow.FitView` share `ControlButtonState`, what
their `className`, `style` and `render` receive:

| field | type | description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `disabled` | `boolean` | the engine refuses its action now (`!engine.can(…)`: a zoom at its limit, `fit-view` before a size), or its `disabled` prop |

## Data attributes

| attribute | value |
| --- | --- |
| `data-flow-part` | `"controls"` |

The buttons carry their own parts (`zoom-in`, `zoom-out`, `fit-view`, `toggle-interactive`).
